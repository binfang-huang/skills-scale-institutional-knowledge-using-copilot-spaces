## Process Summary

OctoAcme’s process moves from initiation—validating the need, outcomes, stakeholders, and go/no-go decision—to planning, where teams define scope, backlog, estimates, dependencies, risks, milestones, and the Definition of Done. During execution, teams deliver in small increments, track work on a project board, test and review changes, communicate progress, and escalate blockers. Releases require acceptance criteria, passing checks, release notes, and rollback preparation, followed by deployment verification and stakeholder communication. Teams then capture learnings in retrospectives and track prioritized improvement actions. Risk management and clear role ownership apply throughout the lifecycle.
